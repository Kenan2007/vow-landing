The ritual

Declare. Sign. Get held to it.

01
Declare

Say it in your own words.

By voice or text. "I want to run three times a week." Vow asks when, what stops you, and the smallest action that counts as started.

02
Sign

Sign the contract.

Your answers become a commitment contract. You sign it with your name. The promise stops being a thought and becomes binding to self.

03
Check in

Vow reaches out first.

Every morning at your time, a push, text or voice note: "Yesterday you said you'd run. Talk to me." It never waits for you.

Learn
